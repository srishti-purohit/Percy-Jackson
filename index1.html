Percy Jackson and the Olympians

Rick Riordan's series is about a kid who keeps getting expelled from schools, finds out the Greek gods are very real, and learns that his dad happens to be one of them. It opens with a stolen lightning bolt and a road trip across America, and it somehow gets bigger, funnier and more emotional with every book. The first one, The Lightning Thief, came out in 2005 and the story has since grown into more books, a film series and a TV adaptation.

The idea

The gods of Olympus never actually went away. They moved with Western civilization, so right now Mount Olympus floats above the Empire State Building, the entrance to the Underworld is in Los Angeles, and the gods still have children with mortals. Those children are demigods, and monsters start hunting them the moment their powers begin to show. Percy is one of them, and the dyslexia and ADHD he struggles with at school turn out to be signs of a brain wired for ancient Greek and battle reflexes.

What makes the books work is the voice. Percy tells everything himself, and he is sarcastic, loyal and constantly in over his head, so even the scariest chapters have a joke somewhere in them.

Camp Half-Blood

Camp Half-Blood sits on Long Island, and it is the one place where demigods are meant to be safe. There is a cabin for each Olympian, a climbing wall that spits lava, strawberry fields, a sword fighting arena and the Big House, where the camp is run from. Campers train all summer, play capture the flag in the woods and wait for the quests that nobody really wants to be sent on.

Percy Jackson

A son of Poseidon and the narrator of the series. His pen turns into a sword named Riptide, and he can do surprising things with water.

Annabeth Chase

A daughter of Athena who loves architecture and always seems to be three steps ahead of everyone, Percy especially. She is the strategist of every quest.

Grover Underwood

A satyr, Percy's best friend and his protector at school. He is deeply committed to nature and has strong feelings about tin cans.

Chiron

A centaur who has trained heroes for thousands of years and who looks after the campers.

Thalia Grace

A daughter of Zeus who is tied to Annabeth's past and later becomes a lieutenant of the Hunters of Artemis.

Luke Castellan

A son of Hermes who is tied to Annabeth's past and ends up being the series' main antagonist.

The five books

1. The Lightning Thief

Percy is accused of stealing Zeus's master bolt, and he heads west with Annabeth and Grover to clear his name and get his mother back from the Underworld.

2. The Sea of Monsters

Camp is in danger, so Percy, Annabeth and his half brother Tyson sail through the Sea of Monsters to find the Golden Fleece.

3. The Titan's Curse

Artemis goes missing and Annabeth is in trouble, which pulls Percy into a quest alongside Thalia and the Hunters of Artemis.

4. The Battle of the Labyrinth

Luke's army is searching for a way into camp through Daedalus's Labyrinth, and Percy has to go in first to stop them.

5. The Last Olympian

The war with Kronos reaches Manhattan, and the demigods have to defend Olympus while Percy's prophecy finally comes due.
